# Quick Start and Daily Use

## One-time arrangement

1. Launch StatusPerch.
2. Hold the Command key.
3. Drag the slim divider to the right of the items you want to collect.
4. Keep the StatusPerch chevron immediately to the right of the divider.
5. Keep the clock and frequently used system controls to the right of the chevron.

Recommended order:

`occasional apps → divider → chevron → clock and system controls`

macOS saves the order. StatusPerch does not read the names or contents of the items.

## Hide and show

- Click the chevron to hide items on the left of the divider.
- Click it again to show them.
- Right-click the chevron for settings.
- Click the divider for settings.
- Command-drag either StatusPerch control to move it.

## Reorder collected items

Expand first, hold Command, then drag any menu-bar item to its preferred location. The order is controlled and saved by macOS.

## Quit safely

Choose Quit StatusPerch from the menu. The app restores the expanded divider width before exiting so collected items become visible again.

## Multiple displays

Keep the chevron and divider on the same menu bar. If they are separated across displays or reversed, StatusPerch warns instead of collapsing unpredictably.
