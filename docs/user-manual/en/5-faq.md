# Frequently Asked Questions

## Does StatusPerch record my screen?

No. It does not request or use Screen Recording.

## Does it need Accessibility or Input Monitoring?

No. You perform ordering through the native Command-drag behavior provided by macOS.

## Does it connect to the internet?

No. Version 1.0.0 has no account, analytics, advertising, update network client, or cloud service.

## Can it automatically identify and sort other apps' menu-bar items?

No. macOS does not provide a public API for that behavior, and StatusPerch avoids invasive workarounds.

## Can it display a second menu-bar row?

No. Products that show an extra row use a separate floating panel; they are not changing the system menu bar into two rows.

## Why is there a divider?

The divider marks the boundary. Items to its left are collected; the chevron and always-visible items stay to its right. Its width is also the mechanism used to hide or show the collected area.

## Why is the app not in the Dock?

StatusPerch is a menu-bar accessory. Use its chevron or divider to open settings and quit.

## Is it open source?

No. The public repository contains documentation and release binaries only. The application and brand are proprietary freeware.
